How to read this

  • Startup tags are a judgment call. No source flags startups. Startup means new-age, venture or PE backed, and platform led.
  • The tech list is built from Yahoo's sector plus a hand-picked add of new-age names Yahoo labels differently, such as Zomato, PB Fintech, Groww, Lenskart, PhysicsWallah and Delhivery. Ola Electric and Ather are left out as vehicle makers.
  • Stock analysis uses reported data only: Yahoo's business description and trailing-twelve-month figures. The commentary is generated from those numbers by fixed rules. It describes what the numbers show and is not a recommendation.
  • SME tech is classified from business descriptions on Chittorgarh, then pruned by hand. Some SME rows have no split history, so their gains are not split-adjusted. A split would make the true gain higher.
